Jaipuria and the World

At Jaipuria Institute of Management, we believe that education must go beyond borders. In today's interconnected world, global exposure is not just an advantage; it is a necessity. Our International Relations Office is dedicated to fostering global partnerships and providing our students with a truly international perspective.

We aim to create future-ready leaders who can navigate diverse cultures and complex global business environments. Through our extensive network of partner universities, we offer a plethora of opportunities for students to study abroad, engage with international faculty, and participate in cross-cultural research projects.

Join us to expand your horizons and embrace a global mindset that will set you apart in your professional journey.

Expanding Horizons, Forging Global Ties

Students Going Global

Trimester Exchange Programmes

Students have the opportunity to spend a full trimester at one of our esteemed partner universities across the globe. This immersive experience allows them to study international curriculum, interact with diverse peer groups, and gain a profound understanding of global business dynamics. Credits earned are seamlessly transferred, ensuring academic continuity.

Short-Term International Immersion Programmes

For those seeking condensed yet impactful global exposure, our short-term immersion programs are ideal. Ranging from 2 to 4 weeks, these programs combine academic sessions, industry visits, and cultural excursions. They are designed to provide a rapid yet deep dive into the business practices and cultural nuances of a foreign country.

The World Coming to Jaipuria

International Visiting Faculty

At Jaipuria, internationalization is a two-way street. We regularly host distinguished faculty from our global partner institutions to teach full modules or conduct specialized workshops. This ensures that every student on campus benefits from international perspectives, diverse teaching methodologies, and global best practices, even without traveling abroad.
