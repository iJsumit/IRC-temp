About COIR

Connecting Cultures. Enabling Global Learning.

The Central Office of International Relations (COIR) serves as a strategic unit dedicated to advancing the institution’s vision of global engagement and internationalization.

It aims to provide students and faculty with meaningful international exposure through academic collaboration, cross-cultural interaction, and global learning opportunities. India acts as an attractive destination for exchange, combining academic rigour with extraordinary cultural richness.

Our Core Commitments:

  • Enhancing intercultural competence and global awareness
  • Enabling transformative learning experiences
  • Exchange programmes, international faculty interaction & diverse engagement

Destination India

India functions as a live laboratory. With rapid digital transformation, students gain first-hand insights into emerging markets and frugal innovation.
