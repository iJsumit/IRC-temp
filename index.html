Jaipuria Global Immersion

Connecting our campuses to the world beyond.

Jaipuria and the World

At Jaipuria Institute of Management, one of India's Top-Ranked Business Schools, the Central Office of International Relations (COIR) is the force that connects our campuses in Lucknow, Noida, Jaipur, and Indore to the world beyond. We are built on a singular conviction: that truly great management education does not end at the campus gate. It extends across cultures, continents, and communities.

Through a growing network of partnerships spanning Europe, Asia, Oceania, and the Middle East, COIR powers a full spectrum of global engagement, including student exchanges, international immersions, inbound programmes, faculty collaborations, virtual learning, and research partnerships. Every initiative is designed to ensure that every Jaipuria student and faculty member has access to experiences that are genuinely, irreversibly transformative.

Our commitment rests on three pillars: building intercultural competence, cultivating global awareness, and enabling transformative learning that prepares graduates to lead with clarity and confidence in an interconnected world.

International Academic Collaborations

Students Going Global

Trimester Exchange Programme

For students who are ready to experience the world as their classroom, Jaipuria offers the opportunity to spend a full academic trimester at a prestigious international partner university, living on campus, learning from world-renowned faculty, and collaborating with peers drawn from across the globe.

It is not just an academic credit. It is a trimester that expands perspective, deepens confidence, and builds the kind of global networks that last a lifetime.

Short-Term International Immersion Programmes

Not every transformation requires months. Jaipuria's immersion programmes are intensive, purpose-built experiences that take students to the heart of global business, blending academic rigour, industry exposure, and cultural depth into journeys that are compact in time but expansive in impact.

Across multiple global destinations every year, our students do not just observe international business environments. They step inside them.

Global Academic Partners

Active MoUs with prestigious institutions worldwide for trimester exchanges, collaborative research, and global learning.

The World Coming to Jaipuria

Student Inbound Programmes

Jaipuria's campuses are not just places where our students go global. They are destinations where the world comes to learn. Through structured inbound programmes, we welcome international students for short-term immersions, semester exchanges, and customised academic modules that offer a rich encounter with India's business landscape, cultural heritage, and socio-economic diversity.

Comprehensive support, including onboarding, accommodation, and cultural integration, ensures that every visiting student feels at home from day one and leaves transformed.

Global Learning, Right Here on Campus

International Teaching Week

Distinguished faculty from our partner universities across the world are invited to our campuses to deliver full credit courses, bringing global pedagogy, international case studies, and world-class expertise directly into the Jaipuria classroom. Every session is a window into how the world thinks, teaches, and leads.

Inbound Faculty Exchange

International faculty from partner institutions join our campuses to deliver academic modules across disciplines, enriching student learning with diverse perspectives, global frameworks, and teaching approaches that go far beyond the conventional.

Modular Teaching by Visiting International Faculty

Leading professors from global institutions conduct focused, specialised academic sessions that sharpen students' understanding of contemporary global business challenges, bringing the knowledge of international management education to our campuses.

Expert Sessions and Research Webinars

From guest lectures and Faculty Development Programmes to prestigious research seminars under the FT50 Star Series, Jaipuria regularly connects its students and faculty with leading academic and industry minds from institutions across North America, Europe, Asia, and Australia. Every session is an invitation to think bigger, question deeper, and reach further.

Collaborative Online International Learning (COIL)

Through COIL, Jaipuria students and faculty collaborate virtually with peers from partner institutions around the world, co-creating knowledge through joint assignments, shared case discussions, and cross-border academic projects. Global collaboration is no longer an exception at Jaipuria. It is a way of learning

Our Faculty on the World Stage

Outbound Faculty Engagement

When Jaipuria faculty step onto international campuses as visiting professors, exchange scholars, or academic collaborators, they return carrying something invaluable: a broader view of the world that finds its way into every lecture, every research paper, and every student they mentor. Through active outbound faculty engagement at partner institutions across the globe, Jaipuria ensures that our classrooms are always informed by the world's best thinking.
