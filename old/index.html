Jaipuria and the World

At Jaipuria Institute of Management, one of India's Top-Ranked Business Schools, the Central Office of International Relations (COIR) is the force that connects our campuses in Lucknow, Noida, Jaipur, and Indore to the world beyond. We are built on a singular conviction: that truly great management education does not end at the campus gate. It extends across cultures, continents, and communities.

Through a growing network of partnerships spanning Europe, Asia, Oceania, and the Middle East, COIR powers a full spectrum of global engagement, including student exchanges, international immersions, inbound programmes, faculty collaborations, virtual learning, and research partnerships. Every initiative is designed to ensure that every Jaipuria student and faculty member has access to experiences that are genuinely, irreversibly transformative.

Our commitment rests on three pillars: building intercultural competence, cultivating global awareness, and enabling transformative learning that prepares graduates to lead with clarity and confidence in an interconnected world.

Global Academic Partners

Active MoUs with prestigious institutions worldwide for trimester exchanges, collaborative research, and global learning.

Outbound Programmes

Flagship Program

Trimester Exchange Programme

A three-month immersive experience at Rennes School of Business, France. Participants spend a full trimester at the institution, with their academic evaluation conducted by the host university.

Global Exposure

Immersion Programs

Transformative learning experiences through partnerships with global universities such as Hult Business School (Dubai), College de Paris (France), Munich Business School (Germany) and NSBM Green University (Sri Lanka). These blend academic sessions, cultural interactions, and industry visits to enhance readiness for dynamic professional environments.

Expert Insights

Global Guest Sessions & Webinars

We host guest sessions and webinars led by prominent international experts and academics. These sessions provide students with fresh perspectives on global trends and emerging management challenges, bridging classroom learning with real-world practices.

Academic Excellence

Faculty Exchange Programs

Connecting educators with global institutions like TAR University (Malaysia) and Weltech & Whitireia (New Zealand). These programs foster cross-disciplinary research, innovation in teaching, and sharing of best practices with international peers, reflecting our commitment to global collaboration.
