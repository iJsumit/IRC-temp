Central Office of International Relations

Connecting Cultures. Enabling Global Learning.

The Central Office of International Relations (COIR) serves as a strategic unit dedicated to advancing the institution’s vision of global engagement and internationalization. It aims to provide students and faculty with meaningful international exposure through academic collaboration, cross-cultural interaction, and global learning opportunities.

  • Enhancing intercultural competence and global awareness
  • Enabling transformative learning experiences
  • Through exchange programmes, international faculty interaction, and diverse student engagement

Destination India

India functions as a live laboratory. With rapid digital transformation, students gain first-hand insights into emerging markets.
